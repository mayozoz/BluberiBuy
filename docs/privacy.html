BluberiBuy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

BluberiBuy is a Chrome extension that tracks prices on luxury fashion sites. This policy explains what information the extension handles, where it goes, and how to remove it.

In short: your tracked items, price history, and settings stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us unless you turn on email alerts — then your email address and the alert details are sent so the alert can be delivered. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics, and we never sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It never leaves your device unless described below:

  • Items you track: product link, name, brand, image link, store, prices, price history, and stock status
  • Your folders, target prices, and alert preferences
  • Your settings, including the email address you enter for alerts

Price checks on supported stores

To keep prices up to date, the extension loads the product pages of items you track, directly from the store (SSENSE, The RealReal, Fashionphile, The Outnet, and Vestiaire Collective), both in the background and when you visit those pages. These requests use your browser's existing cookies for that store, the same as a normal visit. They go straight from your browser to the store. We don't receive them, and the extension doesn't send the store anything about your other tracked items.

Email alerts (optional)

Email alerts are off unless you enter an email address and turn on email for an item. When an alert is triggered, the extension sends the following to EmailJS, the email service we use to deliver it:

  • Your email address
  • The alert itself: item name, the price change or stock change, and the product link

EmailJS delivers the alert from our sending account, bluberibuy@gmail.com (a Gmail account). Because of how sending works, a copy of each alert, including your address, is kept in that account's sent mail and in EmailJS's sending history. We use these only to deliver and troubleshoot alerts. EmailJS handles this data under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data, and no use of it for anything other than the features above
  • No access to sites other than the supported stores

The use of information received by BluberiBuy adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Stop email alerts: turn off the email toggles on your items, or clear the address in Settings.
  • Delete everything on your device: uninstall the extension. Chrome removes its stored data.
  • Delete sent alert copies: email bluberibuy@gmail.com from the address that received alerts, and we'll delete the copies in our sending account and EmailJS history.

Changes to this policy

If the extension starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
